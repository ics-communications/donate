People Need Places Where Faith and Learning Grow Together

We live in a divided world where suspicion, exclusion, and cultural pressure strain communities, institutions, and even churches.

Many Christians are looking for something deeper: a faith that is spiritually grounded, intellectually serious, and spacious enough for honest questions, meaningful relationships, and people who need a place to belong.

A Community Forming Faithful Leaders

Since 1967, the Institute for Christian Studies has been a community where people can deepen their Christian imagination, strengthen their engagement with the world, and prepare for faithful service to the common good.

Through faith-based graduate education, research, and public learning, students and community members encounter a faith that engages the questions of our time while remaining rooted in grace, humility, justice, and hope.

This Community Needs People Who Sustain It

The work of formation is ongoing. New students arrive, graduates continue into lives of service, and people return to learn, wrestle with new questions, and reconnect with a community that has helped shape them. And there are always others still searching for a place where faith, learning, and belonging can grow together.

That’s why monthly supporters are so important.

Our monthly supporters are people like you who know firsthand how meaningful a community like ICS can be. By giving each month, you can ensure others have that same place to learn, belong, ask honest questions, and grow in faith.

Your dependable support helps ICS welcome people today and prepare for those who will arrive tomorrow.

Welcome People Into the Community

Your monthly support helps provide scholarships that make rigorous and hospitable Christian learning accessible to people seeking a place to grow in faith and wisdom.

Form Leaders for Years to Come

Your ongoing giving supports the careful work of educating and mentoring teachers, pastors, and public leaders who will serve their communities for decades.

Sustain the Work for the Future

Recurring gifts provide the stability ICS needs to strengthen student support, expand public learning, and maintain the people and infrastructure that make this community possible.
