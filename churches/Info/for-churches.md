# A Companion for Your *Congregation*

Leading a church today is heavy work. We've compiled these resources from the ICS community for to help you and your congregation tackle the most pressing issues facing Christians today.

## What ICS Offers Churches

The Institute for Christian Studies has walked alongside churches for more than fifty years. We've listened to many congregants describe what they are wrestling with now: polarization, anxiety, and hard conversations, to name just a few. Over the years, we've hosted conversations, curated events, and created resources, and we wanted to share them with you and your church to help navigate our world.

## Justice & Faith

*Research and a play on congregations pursuing justice*

[Watch the Justice and Faith play](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1o6F-q_P0mw)

A collaborative research project exploring how Christian Reformed congregations understand and pursue justice, developed with the Centre for Community Based Research. The play performed as part of the project brings its findings to life, and both are offered here for your church to watch, read, and discuss.

[Read the Research](https://www.icscanada.edu/research)
